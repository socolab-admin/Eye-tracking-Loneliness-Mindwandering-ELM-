## Eye–fNIRS Synchronization

The synchronized eye–fNIRS datasets were generated by aligning the processed eye-tracking and fNIRS recordings to a common time base established through Lab Streaming Layer (LSL) during data acquisition. Synchronization was enabled by shared event markers transmitted from the PsychoPy experiment via LSL and recorded in both the raw EyeLink (.edf) and fNIRS trigger (.tri) files, providing a common temporal reference across modalities.

### Synchronization Procedure

1. **Pair corresponding recordings**

   * **Video-Viewing:** Eye-tracking and fNIRS recordings were matched by participant and video stimulus.
   * **SART:** Eye-tracking and fNIRS recordings were matched by participant.

2. **Verify recording durations**

   * Recording durations were computed independently within subject, between modalities and compared to ensure successful alignment.
   * Duration summaries are available in `data/qc/sync/`.

3. **Normalize recording time**

   * fNIRS recording time was rebased such that the first sample corresponded to **0 seconds** (onset of video or SART trial).
   * Eye-tracking timestamps (`t_rel_ms`) were converted from milliseconds to seconds and similarly referenced to the beginning of the recording.

4. **Downsample eye-tracking to the fNIRS sampling rate**

   * Eye-tracking recordings (~1000 Hz) were synchronized to the fNIRS sampling frequency (~9.765 Hz).
   * Each eye-tracking sample was assigned to its corresponding fNIRS acquisition interval using the fNIRS sampling timestamps.

5. **Aggregate eye-tracking measures**

   * For every fNIRS sample, eye-tracking measures recorded within the corresponding sampling interval were summarized by computing:

     * Mean binocular horizontal gaze position (`xp`)
     * Mean binocular vertical gaze position (`yp`)
     * Mean binocular pupil size (`ps`)
     * Mean interpolated left/right gaze coordinates (`xpl_i`, `xpr_i`, `ypl_i`, `ypr_i`)
     * Mean interpolated left/right pupil sizes (`psl_i`, `psr_i`)
     * Blink proportion (`blink_prop`)
     * Number of contributing eye-tracking samples (`eye_n`)

6. **Generate synchronized datasets**

   * The aggregated eye-tracking measures were merged with each fNIRS sample, producing synchronized multimodal datasets for both the **Video-Viewing** and **SART** paradigms.
